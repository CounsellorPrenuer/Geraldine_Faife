About Founder

Ms. Geraldine Faife is an educationalist with more than thirty-eight years of experience in nurturing children and young adults in national and international schools and institutions. She is driven to see her students and clients reach their full potential and live happier, fulfilled lives. As a dedicated Career Counsellor, she empowers individuals to discover their strengths, drive and career aspirations, through personalized guidance and expert support. With a compassionate and non-judgmental approach, she creates a safe space for individuals to explore their career aspirations and overcome obstacles.
